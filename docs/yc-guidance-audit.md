# Audit of the supplied YC advice

Checked 26 October 2026 against the official sources below. The user's notes are useful preparation prompts, but they are not a definitive transcript of YC's scoring rubric or every application's requirements.

| Supplied assertion | Assessment | Working guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YC tests clarity, velocity, domain insight and chemistry | Useful internal framing, not a verified exhaustive official rubric | Use the four lenses to inspect evidence; do not attribute them as an official scorecard |
| Every essential question is known in advance | Unsupported universal claim | Prepare facts and understand the business; unexpected questions remain possible |
| The CEO must answer about 70% and always answer first | No such requirement in reviewed official guidance | Share topics naturally and allow the knowledgeable founder to answer; every founder should be ready |
| Every numerical fact must be memorized instantly | Conflicts with official interview advice allowing a metrics reference | Know definitions and material figures; keep the fact sheet nearby |
| Extensive mock interviews and scripted answers are necessary | Official guide discourages rehearsal | Prepare understanding and artifacts; spend time improving the company |
| Equal equity is mandatory; unequal splits automatically reject | Overstated | YC advocates equal or near-equal splits; that is strong advice, not an automatic eligibility rule |
| Solo founders are rejected | Incorrect | YC's FAQ says solo founders are accepted |
| A nontechnical founder is automatically disqualified | Overstated | The FAQ emphasizes the founding team's ability to build the product; explain actual technical ownership |
| The product demo must be raw, unedited and exactly 60 seconds | Not established by reviewed public guidance | Follow the current demo field instructions; a concise working demonstration is our recommendation |
| The one-minute application video should show the product | Incorrect for the founder video | That video is founders speaking; the demo belongs in its separate field |
| 500+ FPS, under-one-hour RL and zero-shot robot transfer define a YC PoC | Proposed targets, not YC application requirements | Pick evidence suited to the current thesis and available hardware |
| A polished dashboard, website or billing system is needed | Unnecessary for this technical milestone | Build the smallest artifact that supports the claim and a real user workflow |
| Three beta labs, customers or market numbers can illustrate a draft | Only if explicitly labeled hypothetical | Never transfer illustrative claims into factual application answers |
| Missing revenue or robot transfer means it is too early | Incorrect as an eligibility rule | YC considers idea-stage teams; describe limits clearly and keep making progress |

## Official facts used in this repository

- Founder video: one minute, founders speaking, all founders included, spontaneous rather than recited. Product demo is separate. [YC video](https://www.ycombinator.com/video).
- Interview: ten-minute Zoom conversation; all founders present; a working demo may be requested. Natural answers, progress and familiarity with users matter. [YC interviews](https://www.ycombinator.com/interviews).
- Application stage: idea-stage and solo-founder applications are considered; founding technical capability matters. [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq).
- Equity: equal or close-to-equal splits are recommended. The advice discusses motivation and future contribution; it is not a categorical rejection policy. [Michael Seibel, equity splits](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/splitting-equity-among-founders/).
- Current timing: Winter 2027 on-time deadline 2 November 2026 at 8 p.m. Pacific. Recheck before submission. [YC apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply).

## Boundaries

We have not inspected a signed-in application form, submitted an application, contacted YC, verified private company facts or secured customer commitments. Live form instructions take precedence over an internal storyboard. The preparation materials are designed to clarify ability through inspectable work; they do not predict acceptance.
